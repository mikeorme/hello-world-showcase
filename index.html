Chapter 1 · The Brief

It was supposed to be the simplest page on the internet.

“Make the most amazing generic ‘hello world’ homepage that you can muster.”The brief

Then came the words that change everything: a company-wide effort. A tireless, multi-month push, with multiple cross-organizing teams. On paper, one sentence. In the legend, a conference room that never emptied.

Chapter 2 · The Rejected Draft

The first version had confetti.

It also had a fake day-counter, a nine-version changelog, and an elaborate joke about the process that produced it. The room loved it for almost a full minute.

“The confetti is lame.”Leadership, reviewing draft one

The deeper note was fairer: the page was making fun of the work instead of delivering the message. Focus on the message. Level up the craft.

Chapter 3 · The Pivot

So they went to the GPU.

A hand-written shader painting domain-warped noise on a single triangle. Letters that swell toward your cursor, one variable-font weight at a time. Nothing else on the page. Leadership approved.
